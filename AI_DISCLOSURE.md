# AI Assistance Disclosure

This file records how AI assistance was used in this repository, so users, pack maintainers and
downstream redistributors can judge the contribution.

## Tool and model

- **Agent:** OpenCode coding agent
- **Model:** DeepSeek V4.1 Flash
- **Session:** supervised by the repository owner, NeoRussia

## Scope of AI assistance

AI assistance was used for:

- the concrete metadata refactor: grouping cube and slab textures into metadata families,
  generating the slab `variant` / `half` blockstates, and keeping the block id count low;
- the HL Blocks integration: simple blocks, metadata groups, shift-scroll cycling, ore dictionary
  registration and Chisel carving groups;
- the concrete recipes and the `concrete` / `blockConcrete` ore dictionary registration;
- debugging the single-slab model lookup and reordering the slab blockstate keys to
  `half=...,variant=...`;
- the Thermite Charge integration: the supplied 3D model and sprite, face-inset mounting, fuse,
  breach and sound/particle logic, and the matching door breach rendering and collision;
- the Redstone Gate relay linking: gate-to-gate and gate-to-door sessions, mirrored outputs and
  door redstone integration;
- the Redstone Monocle: its supplied 16x16 sprite, the client-side wireless-link overlay, the
  four-chunk drawing limit and the owner-only security-door Redstone toggle, including the
  GL-state restoration fix;
- the office-door colour skin sets and their 32x64 texture downscales;
- the 3x3 office-door models and item models, scaled from the 2x2 double-door assets;
- the Office Door 1/2/3 crafting recipes, including their locked and security variants;
- the Blast Shutter, Water Door, Sliding Seal, Cargo Door and QE sliding door recipes, including
  their locked and security upgrades;
- the remaining vault, blast, fire, secure access, airlock, silo hatch, vehicle, containment,
  transition seal and test door recipes, including their locked and security upgrades;
- the Spatial Disrupter creative-break exception;
- a Creative Relay Key prototype that was removed before release at the owner's request;
- the Keycard Programmer page-two layout, including the six-column, four-row card palette;
- UAC Keycards and their creative-tab and programmer-palette entries;
- the O5 Keycard, using the supplied 16x16 sprite and fixed tooltip, with its creative-tab and
  programmer-palette entries;
- the Keycard Holder two-section storage: 16 keycards, 16 relay/utility keys, separate slot
  routing, relay-key ghosts and the `Also holds Relay Keys` tooltip;
- the Breaker Relay Key confirmation flow, server-side ownership and reach validation, security
  door drops, safe ownership and safe contents dropping;
- the Safe Binding Key owner/access check, so a craftable key cannot start a binding session on
  another player's owned safe;
- the scoped GUI tooltip GL-state save and restore used by the programmer, safes, holder, Terminal
  Backup and Documents Folder;
- monitor-only decorative blocks, including their half-depth collision and recipes;
- flat, shadow-free text rendering in the Documents Folder and Notepad editors;
- the Vault-Tec closing-scrape cue timing and the Documents Folder full-height page-edge strip;
- the Documents Folder UV corner fill that keeps the 3D front face opaque when held;
- the Documents Folder and monitor Notepad dark-mode toggles, including the item-NBT and
  desk-saved themes, the immediate client-side handoff and the physical-screen mirror;
- the Emancipation Grill console and field: field modes, colour palettes, redstone event outputs,
  the JEI/HEI filter overlay, player/mob/dropped-item inventory filtering, recursive container
  scanning, and the fizzler integration;
- the repository formatting standards pack and the formatting cleanup of touched files;
- final housekeeping: asset reference validation, stale comment and documentation cleanup,
  BOM removal, item-icon correction and release docs;
- this disclosure and the concrete/HL Blocks section of `README.md`.

## What was not generated by AI

- No textures, models, sounds or other media in this repository were generated by AI, except for
  the office-door assets and their AI-assisted colour variants, 32x64 texture downscales and 3x3
  scaled models noted above. Those are original recreations inspired by the Black Mesa/Half-Life
  office-door style and are released under this project's LGPL-3.0 license; no Black Mesa or
  Half-Life game assets are extracted. The pre-existing HBM door and concrete assets come from
  HBM's Nuclear Tech Mod / NTM CE, and the HL Blocks assets come from NeoRussia's HL Blocks
  project, as listed in `NOTICE`.
- No AI co-author trailers are added to commits. Git history records the human author only.

## Human review

NeoRussia reviewed the AI-assisted changes, built the mod with the repository's Gradle setup, and
tested the concrete, slab, stair, Chisel, ore dictionary, recipe, HL Block, Thermite Charge,
office-door skin and 3x3 door, Redstone Monocle, safe and door-breach behaviour in game, including
the slab model fix. The security equipment and Keycard Holder changes are also built and checked
against a dedicated-server load. The Emancipation Grill field modes and inventory filter were tested
in game by the repository owner. The repository owner owns the code, can explain it, and is
responsible for supporting it.

If you redistribute this mod or a pack containing it, keep this disclosure with the sources.
