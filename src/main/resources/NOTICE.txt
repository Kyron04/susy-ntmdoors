NTM Doors includes assets copied from HBM's Nuclear Tech Mod.

Source: https://github.com/HbmMods/Hbm-s-Nuclear-Tech-GIT
Assets copied: multiblock door OBJ models, door textures, the vault label, the door key and
padlock item icons, and door sound effects.
License: GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0 (see LICENSE.LESSER in this JAR).

The Java implementation in com.ntmdoors is a standalone 1.12.2 port and keeps the original
door behaviour and asset layout where practical.
